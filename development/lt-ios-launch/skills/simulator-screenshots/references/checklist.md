# Screenshot checklist

- [ ] Image is from the intended Simulator and current build.
- [ ] Status bar and safe areas are not cropped unintentionally.
- [ ] The app occupies most of the card.
- [ ] Headline is one line and readable at thumbnail scale.
- [ ] Marketing copy matches the visible state.
- [ ] No unsupported performance, health, popularity, or pricing claim.
- [ ] All three files share dimensions, background, type, and spacing.
- [ ] Raw and composed PNGs are 8-bit RGB and contain no alpha channel.
- [ ] Raw and composed files are both retained.
